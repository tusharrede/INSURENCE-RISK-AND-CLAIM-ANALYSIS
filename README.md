# Insurance Risk and Claim Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Description
The Insurance Risk and Claim Analysis Dashboard is a data analytics and business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The main objective of this project is to analyze insurance policy data, evaluate premium contributions, monitor claim statuses, and understand policy renewal trends through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key insurance business metrics, helping users understand policy distribution, compare regional premium performance, and identify patterns in claims and renewals.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze the total number of insurance policies and key financial metrics.
- Understand policy distribution across Health, Motor, Life, Travel, and Home insurance.
- Compare total premium amounts across different states.
- Monitor claim status categories, including Approved, Rejected, Under Review, and No Claim.
- Analyze city-wise premium contributions.
- Evaluate policy renewal status to understand renewal patterns.
- Present meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.

## Dashboard Features
1. **Policy Analysis:** Visualizes the number of policies across different insurance categories to identify the most and least common policy types.
2. **State-wise Premium Analysis:** Compares premium contributions across states to identify regions with higher premium values.
3. **Policy Type Distribution:** Uses a pie chart to display the distribution of policies by insurance category.
4. **Claim Status Analysis:** Shows claim record counts across No Claim, Approved, Under Review, and Rejected statuses.
5. **City-wise Premium Analysis:** Compares premium amounts across cities to explore regional business performance.
6. **Renewal Status Analysis:** Displays policies classified as Renewed, Due Soon, Not Renewed, and Not Applicable.
7. **Interactive Filtering:** Includes a Claim Status slicer to filter and explore the report based on selected categories.
